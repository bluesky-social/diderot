Ask in Discord

Ask answers where you turn it on: a mention starts a thread with the answer, /ask asks (privately with private:True), and the message commands Summarize thread and Summarize thread privately summarize a thread. An answer in a channel uses only public content and the chosen channels everyone in it can read in Discord; a direct message or a private answer, what its asker can read.
