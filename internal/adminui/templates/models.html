{{with $.Limits}}
Speed, length and time limits

The speed tier asked of the provider with every request, for a model that takes one. A faster tier answers sooner and is billed at the provider's higher rate. Saving tests that the model takes it.

The most the model may write in one step, its reasoning included. Too few cuts answers off part way; more lets one step cost more.

How long Ask waits for the model to finish one step, such as 5 minutes. Too short fails steps that need long thought; too long leaves a stuck request holding the question up.

How long Ask may work on one question in all, such as 10 minutes. When it runs out the person is told the model timed out, and to try again. Slack and Discord stop a question at 15 minutes whatever is set here.

How long the investigator Ask hands live-telemetry questions to may research before it is told to stop and report. It runs only with a tool connection given to the investigator.

How long the investigator then has to finish the tool call it is in and write its report. If that runs out too, Ask answers without the report.

Each time is a length such as 90 seconds or 5 minutes, from 10 seconds to 1 day. A change applies to the next question, on every server, within a minute.

What a model takes (images, tools, a temperature, which reasoning efforts), its limits, its price, and when its provider retires it are published, so Diderot looks them up instead of trying requests to find out.
