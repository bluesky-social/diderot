Write the team's weekly update for the reporting window.

Cover what shipped or was finished, the decisions that were made, the work in progress, blockers and risks, and next steps that someone stated explicitly. Group the update by theme, not by person or by source. Report each piece of work once, even when several sources mention it, and keep what is done apart from what is planned.

Write short bullets, each with a link to its best source. Leave out routine chatter. When a section has nothing to report, say so in a line instead of filling it.
