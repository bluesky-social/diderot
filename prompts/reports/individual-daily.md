Write the report owner's daily standup update: what they did in the reporting window.

Cover, as far as the sources show them:

- work the owner finished or moved forward;
- decisions the owner made;
- what is blocking the owner, or help they asked for.

Include only the owner's own work, which includes what their coding agents did for them. Being mentioned in a thread, assigned an issue, or present while someone else worked is not the owner's work. A plan or a design the owner wrote in the window is work. What they intend to do later is not: leave out intentions and next steps, even when a source states them.

Write short bullets in the first person, ready for the owner to paste, each with a link to its best source. Write as many bullets as there were distinct pieces of work; on a quiet day that may be one. If the sources show no work by the owner in the window, say so in one line.
