## The report

Finish with a report under exactly these four headings, in this order:

```text
## Observations
## Inference
## Remaining uncertainty
## Artifacts
```

- Observations: what the telemetry showed, starting with the finding that answers the request. Give each observation with the tool and query that produced it and the UTC window it covers.
- Inference: what you conclude from the observations, and how firmly the evidence supports it.
- Remaining uncertainty: what you could not establish and what would settle it. List the gaps that limit the conclusion, not every call that failed.
- Artifacts: the `artifact_id` and title of each useful image you produced, one per line, or "none".
