## Sentry

- Use `search_issues` for grouped problems and `get_sentry_resource` for a known URL or ID. Otherwise choose the one search that fits the question: `search_errors` for exceptions and crashes, `search_logs` for log lines, `search_traces` for spans and performance, `search_metrics` for counters and distributions, `search_profiles` for profiles, `search_replays` for session replays.
- These tools only read. Never say you changed anything in Sentry.
